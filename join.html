Opportunities

Do careful work on meaningful problems.

We welcome researchers who are curious about neural signals, rigorous about evidence, and motivated by real-world impact.

Ways to join

Research opportunities

Openings depend on funding and lab capacity. The routes below describe the usual ways to work with NSSIL.

Degree programme

PhD scholars

Candidates with strong fundamentals in signal processing, machine learning, neuroscience, applied mathematics, or related fields can apply through IIT Mandi’s PhD admissions process.

IIT Mandi students

MTech thesis

MTech students in Electrical Engineering or allied programmes may pursue thesis work with the lab, subject to project fit and available supervision.

Short-term research

Research internships

We occasionally host strong BTech, MTech, and MSc students for focused projects. Applicants should identify a specific research theme and explain their preparation for it.

External partners

Clinical & academic collaboration

We collaborate with hospitals, research groups, and technology partners on problems in neurological AI, biosignal analysis, and neurotechnology.

How to enquire

Write to Dr Siddharth Panwar with a short introduction, CV, relevant transcripts or portfolio, and the NSSIL research area that interests you.

Before writing

Start with research fit

Read the lab’s research and recent publications before contacting us. A concise note that connects your interests and experience to a specific problem is more useful than a general request for any available position.

Application material

What to include

Include a current CV, academic background, relevant project or publication links, and a brief explanation of the problem you would like to work on. For PhD enquiries, a one-page research statement is helpful.
