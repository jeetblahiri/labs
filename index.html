Research

From principled models to practical systems.

Our work combines statistical signal processing, neuroscience, dynamical systems, and compact machine learning. Clinical reliability is a design requirement, not an afterthought.

01

Clinical neural AI

Interpretable EEG biomarkers and evaluation protocols built for variation across patients, devices, and clinical centres.

Learn more →
02

Efficient signal restoration

Compact models that remove physiological artifacts while preserving the neural information clinicians and researchers need.

Learn more →
03

Bio-inspired computation

Balanced recurrent systems and connectome-constrained models for studying efficient, sequential neural computation.

Learn more →
04

Dynamics & information

Nonlinear and information-theoretic tools for characterising multivariate brain activity and state transitions.
